01

Problem

A technical catalogue with a people gap

What IT teams were missing

  • TrainingIT's catalogue was technical: Java, Linux, data, AI and certifications.
  • Its buyers, HR and team leads at IT companies, kept raising problems no technical course solves: feedback, leadership, workload and team climate.
  • Generic soft-skills training exists everywhere, and IT professionals reject it because it speaks the language of business, not of their work.

Business goal

Open a new course line that brings soft skills into the catalogue without leaving TrainingIT's technical identity: particular skills with a clear synergy with the IT field, so each course sells to HR and holds the attention of the engineer in the room.

What we mean by soft skills

Personal attributes and interpersonal skills that let a person work effectively with others.

02

Discovery

Research, problems, benchmark

Research summary

Method

Desk research on the challenges IT professionals face at work and a needs analysis focused on communication and organisation, followed by interviews with IT professionals, HR professionals and IT company heads.

Participants

IT professionals, who know where the working day breaks down; HR, who see the gaps in reviews and exits; and company heads, who decide what gets funded.

Key finding

Soft skills land with IT professionals when the lesson starts inside a technical situation they recognise, such as a sprint, a review or a deadline, rather than beside it.

Four problems in IT

Communication is crucial in IT, but professionals struggle to explain technical concepts to non-technical stakeholders and to collaborate with team members from different backgrounds.

Design problemsWhat made it hard

A sceptical room

IT professionals dismiss soft-skills training on sight. The hard-skill frame is what earns their attention.

Urgent versus desirable

Research produces long wish lists. The synthesis had to separate what companies will fund now from what they agree is important.

Teachers who hold both sides

The format needs teachers fluent in the technical context and in the soft skill.

Two buyers, one course

HR signs and the professional attends. Each course has to sell upward and be worth the participant's time.

Strategic opportunity · hard skills as the vehicle

Start in their work

Each session opens on a technical situation the group recognises, such as a review, a retro or an escalation.

Name the soft skill

The soft skill is introduced as what decides how that situation resolves, so it reads as craft rather than HR content.

Practise and carry back

Exercises use the participants' own cases, so what they learn maps onto next week's work.

Curriculum structure20 hours · 4 modules

Every course targets 20 hours of content in four modules that move from foundations to practice. Where a soft skill is paired with a tool, the modules show how to use both together. Four of the draft syllabi:

Three ways to build a courseNew · transplant · teacher material
Option A

New course

Built from scratch with the teacher, module structure included.

  1. Meetings with the teacher
  2. Course design and course document
  3. Videos, presentations, readings and exercises
  4. Check for 20 hours of content
  5. Video transcripts
  6. Upload to the platform
Option B

Direct transplant

An existing course moved onto TrainingIT as it is.

  1. Check for 20 hours of content
  2. Video transcripts
  3. Videos onto the company Vimeo
  4. Upload to the platform
Option C

Teacher's own material

The teacher brings a finished course.

  1. Check for 20 hours of content
  2. Course sheet from the existing material
  3. Video transcripts
  4. Videos onto the company Vimeo
  5. Upload to the platform
Instructional designADDIE or SAM

ADDIE

Analysis, Design, Development, Implementation and Evaluation. Slower, but thorough and focused on getting it right from the start.

SAM

Successive Approximation Model. Iterative: the steps repeat until the course converges on its final design.

Both were evaluated for Option A courses, where the structure is set together with the teacher.

Production standardsContent, platform, delivery

Content

Videos, readings, case studies, infographics and presentations, adding up to 20 hours or as close as possible. Files follow one naming rule, for example DataStoryTelling_clase_1.mov.

Quality check

Every video is transcribed with AI, so errors can be caught without watching every hour of footage.

Platform

Videos are hosted on a private Vimeo and can't be downloaded or watched without access. Materials go into Moodle, and the team makes the web and course banners.

Practice

Simulations, case studies and role-play, with quizzes for instant feedback and self-assessment.

Community

Moodle discussion forums for every course, and live webinars for some.

ValidationPilot · KPIs · surveys

Pilot

A September pilot with 5 to 10 of the courses, launched as Work Efficiency.

Measures

Completion rate, learner satisfaction and impact on job performance, with surveys after every course.

Continuous improvement

Regular content updates, and new courses added as market trends and needs change.
