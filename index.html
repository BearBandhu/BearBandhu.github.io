03 — About

Who am I?

I am a product designer focused on retention loops, user engagement and learning.I am a game designer focused on gameplay, systems and retention loops. I have experience in a wide range of work, focusing primarily on digital products, video games, gamification systems and education.

I have led teams of 6 to 16 people across design, engineering and content, and owned end-to-end product development from concept through balancing, production and launch. Hands-on in Unity and Unreal Engine, with a Master's in Game and Interactive Media Design from U-tad, Madrid, and a Bachelor's in Industrial & Product Design from SCAD.

My background covers UX/UI, research, prototyping, gameplay and systems design, production and product management, and I taught game design fundamentals as a professor at U-tad. I keep two CVs, one for product design and one for game design.
