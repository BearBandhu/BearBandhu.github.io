03 — About

Who am I?

Dedicated and passionate about the world of gaming. 5+ years working in video games.

Experience as a game designer focused on combat design, enemy and boss design, encounter design and game balancing. I have additional experience across multiple areas of game development — UX/UI, pixel art, animation, prototyping, gameplay, production, product management and research — and I taught game design fundamentals as a professor at UTAD.

I understand the pipeline of most departments in games, and I use that knowledge to respect the complexity and necessity of teamwork and cooperation in game development.
