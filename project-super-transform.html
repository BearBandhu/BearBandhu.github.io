Super
Transform

Super transform is a first person puzzle videogame based on transforming different objects to traverse the rooftops. You will embody an interdimensional guard that has the mission to save the city of Madrid in a fractured reality which has been flooded (yes, now it has a beach) and is under the vengeful attack of the squids.

Design problems

One verb, many puzzles
With transformation as the only tool, difficulty has to come from what is in the room rather than from new mechanics.
Legibility in first person
A puzzle you solve by looking needs the solution visible from the place you are standing.
